====================================================
12.1 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
====================================================

EXISTS AND WORKS: a fully force-based craft (zero kinematic writes; every
effect traced to AddForce/AddForceAtPosition/AddTorque), hard-limit thruster
hardware in true newtons, hover PD with curvature feed-forward and landing
damping, surface-alignment torque with predictive look-ahead (works airborne),
yaw damping, traction/drift profile blending, overcharge charge/burst, energy
budget with per-channel accounting, isotropic quadratic drag, wing downforce
(airborne-capable), ground effect, config export/import with change logging,
computed diagnostics.

EXISTS BUT INCOMPLETE/MISLEADING: several tuning fields are dead headroom
above the throttle=1 hard clamp (hoverMaxThrottle 2.25, roofStabilizerMax-
Throttle 10, minBurstThrottle 10 / maxBurstThrottle 50, forceMultiplier > 1);
priority power distribution is ON but selects nothing because both protect
flags are OFF; roof diagnostics read 0 in edit mode; strafe diagnostics report
installed capacity (2,000,000 N) not usable one-direction force (<=1,000,000 N).

INACTIVE / NOT INSTALLED: WorldAtmosphere (script exists, not in scene ->
Earth defaults), CraftDebugHUD (not in scene), SectionAdaptiveSuspension (not
on craft), boost pads (no force code at all), gravity zones (craft doesn't
implement the receiver interface), SceneBootstrapper's physics materials (test
scene only).

GENUINELY MISSING: physics materials on craft & generated track (engine
defaults, 0.6 friction), any collision/contact behavior code, directional
drag, center of pressure & aerodynamic torque, lift/angle-of-attack, explicit
craft-dimension fields, explicit COM/ballast tooling, per-axis inertia
control, thermal/duty-cycle systems, per-node thruster response times, energy
storage (battery).

BIGGEST SURPRISE: the 10,000 kg / 500 kN "current state" exists ONLY as
unsaved editor tuning - the prefab and scene on disk still hold the older
values. The exported JSON in Downloads is currently the only persistent record
of that tuning (see 12.4).

WHAT BLOCKS THE MEDIUM-BASELINE TUNING PASS: dimension/COM/inertia decisions
(Part 1 items are all implicit today), the dead-headroom throttle fields (they
make Inspector numbers lie), the 4-node hardcode (blocks adding hover nodes),
and diagnostics corrections (12.7) so the numbers you tune against are
trustworthy.
