Here’s the clean V1 track generator piece list:

Track Generator V1 Piece Types

1. Straight
Basic forward track section.
Used for connection, speed build-up, and pacing.

2. Wide Straight
Wider straight section.
Used for recovery, overtaking, and giving the player room after difficult sections.

3. Boost Straight
Straight section with boost pads or speed-focused layout.
Used before big banked turns, jumps, or long speed sections.

4. Banked Curve
Main corner type for the game.
Used for fast left/right turns while maintaining high speed.

Variables:
- Direction: Left / Right
- Angle: Gentle / Medium / Sharp
- Banking: Medium / High
- Radius: Medium / Large

5. Banked Hairpin
Very strong 120–180 degree banked turn.
Used as a major direction change and skill check.
Should be used sparingly.

6. S-Curve
Flowing left-right or right-left curve sequence.
Used for rhythm and control without forcing full slowdown.

7. Chicane
Sharper left-right-left or right-left-right sequence.
Used to break up long high-speed sections and test reaction/control.

8. Jump Ramp
Launch ramp that sends the hovercraft into the air.
Used for air-control gameplay.

9. Air Gap
The empty space between a jump ramp and landing ramp.
Can vary in distance and height.

10. Landing Ramp / Recovery Platform
Reverse-shaped ramp that catches the hovercraft after a jump.
Like a motocross landing ramp.
Used to make landings smooth, skill-based, and less physics-janky.

11. Recovery Straight
Safe straight section after a jump, landing, hairpin, or chaotic section.
Gives the player time to stabilize.

12. Tunnel / Bridge Variant
Visual/environmental variant of Straight, Wide Straight, or Banked Curve.
Not a core gameplay type, just a presentation variant.

And the important generator rule:

Most corners should be banked by default.

The hovercraft is very fast, so flat sharp corners should be avoided in V1 unless they are intentionally used as a slow technical challenge.

Every Jump Ramp should normally be followed by:
Jump Ramp → Air Gap → Landing Ramp / Recovery Platform → Recovery Straight

For V1, the basic flow should look like:

Straight
→ Banked Curve
→ Boost Straight
→ High Banked Curve
→ Wide Straight
→ S-Curve
→ Jump Ramp
→ Air Gap
→ Landing Ramp
→ Recovery Straight
→ Banked Hairpin
→ Wide Straight
